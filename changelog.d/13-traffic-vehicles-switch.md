- Street Traffic: a `Vehicles` chip after the road-source chips. Off, the layer
  spawns no simulated dots and draws the live flow on the roads themselves
  (green free flow, amber slow, a pulsing red glow for jams, dashed dark red
  for closures, the simulation's white where there is no flow), so the view
  reads as a congestion map. The choice persists and travels in share links
  as the `t.v` option (absent means on, so existing links keep their dots),
  and a LIVE OPS region click leaves it alone (JWilksBooth, #13).
