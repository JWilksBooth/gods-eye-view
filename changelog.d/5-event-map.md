- Event Map: a new layer that puts live events and places on the globe as
  constant-size icons (cameras, fires, earthquakes, aircraft, vessels and LIVE
  OPS incidents through per-layer adapters), with a hover card, a right-side
  feed drawer (camera snapshot or video, incident detail with the nearest
  cameras, stream embeds) and clustering. Display → GRID or `G` switches to a
  top-down grid view: an orthographic, north-up, straight-down camera with
  tilt locked, a dimmed base map and a lat/lon graticule with MGRS labels; it
  is remembered per browser and travels in share links (`gv`, flag bit 5)
  (JWilksBooth, #5).
