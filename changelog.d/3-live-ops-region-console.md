- LIVE OPS: a left-rail console for Los Angeles, Sonoma County, the San
  Francisco Bay Area and Las Vegas. One click frames the region, turns on
  cameras, the new Live Incidents layer and traffic, and lists the region's
  headlines. Live Incidents merges keyless agency feeds (CHP CAD, DataSF
  dispatched calls, LAFD alerts, CAL FIRE, Nevada 511 events) through
  `/api/incidents`; a Nevada 511 camera pack adds Las Vegas; `get_incidents`
  and `live_ops_region` join the MCP and voice tools (JWilksBooth, #3).
