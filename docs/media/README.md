## Street Traffic vehicles switch

`traffic-vehicles-on.jpg` and `traffic-vehicles-off.jpg` are two captures of
the Osiris globe canvas at the same view of downtown Los Angeles (camera
1,100 m up, heading 25°, pitch −48°), taken from the running app for the
pull request that added the Street Traffic vehicles switch: the first with
simulated vehicles carrying the live TomTom flow, the second with the flow
drawn on the roads alone. Each carries its attribution strip. Imagery:
Powered by Esri — Source: Esri, Maxar, Earthstar Geographics, and the GIS User
Community. Terrain: Re:Earth / Mapterhorn, CC BY 4.0. Roads: ©
OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL), vector tiles OpenFreeMap © OpenMapTiles,
and TomTom; traffic flow data © TomTom. Rendered with CesiumJS. They are kept
only as project documentation of the layer's two views; the imagery, terrain
and data remain subject to their providers' terms (DATA_SOURCES.md).

draped on Re:Earth terrain. Imagery: Powered by Esri — Source: Esri, Maxar,
